  <h1>Privacy Policy for Distance Travel Diary</h1>
  <p class="updated"><strong>Last updated:</strong> November 24, 2026</p>

  <p>Distance Travel Diary (“the App”) is a local path and distance tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.</p>

  <h2>1. Information We Collect</h2>
  <p>The App is designed to work entirely on your device. It does <strong>not</strong> require an account and does <strong>not</strong> send your location or travel data to any server.</p>
  <p>The following information is stored <strong>only on your phone</strong>:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Location data (GPS coordinates) while tracking is active</li>
    <li>Distance, speed, moving time, and rest time</li>
    <li>Tour records</li>
    <li>Diary entries and map paths</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>2. How Information Is Used</h2>
  <p>All data is used solely to provide the App’s features:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Displaying distance, speed, and time statistics (today, week, month)</li>
    <li>Showing your path on a map</li>
    <li>Maintaining a travel diary and tour history</li>
  </ul>
  <p>No data is used for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.</p>

  <h2>3. Data Storage and Sharing</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>All tracking data remains on your device.</li>
    <li>We do not collect, store, or process your data on any external servers.</li>
    <li>We do not share, sell, or transfer any information to third parties.</li>
    <li>Tracking continues in the background only while you have enabled it, and only for the purpose of recording your path.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>4. Permissions</h2>
  <p>The App requests the following permissions:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Location</strong> (including background location, if enabled): Required to record your path and calculate distance and speed.</li>
    <li><strong>Storage</strong> (if applicable): To save data locally on your device.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings. Doing so will stop tracking.</p>

  <h2>5. Data Retention and Deletion</h2>
  <p>Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can clear your travel history or uninstall the App at any time, which permanently removes all stored data from your device.</p>

  <h2>6. Children’s Privacy</h2>
  <p>The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.</p>

  <h2>7. Changes to This Policy</h2>
  <p>We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the App or on this page with an updated date.</p>

  <h2>8. Contact</h2>
  <p>If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in the GitHub repository.</p>
